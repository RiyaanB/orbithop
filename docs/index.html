    How to play

    1. Tap to launchYour ship circles a planet. Tap anywhereClick or press Space to fly off along the dotted line. On your first hops it turns green when you’d land.
    2. Catch the next orbitFly into the dashed ring of the planet ahead. Aim dead-centre for a PERFECT — chain them for combo points.
    3. Don't lingerOrbits decay — wait too long and you burn up. Dodge asteroids, moons and gravity wells. Grab stars for bonus points.
